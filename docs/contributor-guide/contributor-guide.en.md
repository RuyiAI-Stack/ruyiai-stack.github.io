# Contribution and Collaboration Guide

**Join RuyiAI and help build an open, shared RISC-V AI ecosystem.**

Whether you want to make your first open-source contribution, collaborate with the community on technical work, or join real projects through an internship, you are welcome. Choose the path that best matches your interests, experience, and available time.

## 1. Contribute to Open Source

We welcome every developer interested in RISC-V and AI. Code development, issue reports, testing, documentation improvements, and technical discussion are all valuable contributions that help the project move forward.

### Start with an Open Task

You can pick a task from our **Open Task Issues**, then gradually learn the project and how the community collaborates.

1. **Find a task that interests you.** Browse open tasks in the relevant repositories to understand the background, expected goals, and related discussion.

2. **Share your approach with the community.** Leave a comment on the Issue describing your ideas and willingness to contribute, and discuss the implementation direction with maintainers.

3. **Submit your contribution.** Follow the contribution guidelines of the corresponding repository. Submit code, tests, or documentation through a Pull Request (PR), and explain what changed and how it was verified.

4. **Follow up on review and feedback.** Discuss suggestions with reviewers, refine your submission, and deepen your understanding of the project through collaboration.

If this is your first open-source contribution, start by fixing a small issue, adding a test, or improving a section of documentation.

### Share Your Questions and Ideas

Beyond existing tasks, we also welcome you to open Issues, including:

- **Bug reports:** Report errors, compatibility issues, or performance problems found during use.

- **Feature suggestions:** Propose new features, application scenarios, or improvement directions.

- **Task proposals:** Share development work you would like to pursue, and discuss goals and implementation plans with the community.

- **Documentation and experience improvements:** Point out unclear explanations and help improve tutorials, examples, and usage flows.

Before opening an Issue, please check existing discussions first. When reporting problems, include the runtime environment, reproduction steps, and relevant logs whenever possible. When proposing a new direction, feel free to explain what problem it aims to solve and your initial thoughts on implementation.

**Get started:** [Visit RuyiAI on GitHub](https://github.com/RuyiAI-Stack), open a related repository, browse open tasks, or submit an Issue.

## 2. Collaborate with Us

We welcome **open-source organizations, research institutes, hardware vendors, and universities** to connect with RuyiAI and collaborate on shared technical challenges.

Collaboration can start from a concrete problem, a hardware platform, or a research topic, for example:

- **Joint open-source development:** Co-develop and maintain features around AI compilers, operator libraries, runtimes, and framework adaptation.

- **Hardware enablement and optimization:** Adapt software, analyze performance, and pursue hardware-software co-design for RISC-V chips and computing platforms.

- **Research and engineering collaboration:** Turn research ideas into verifiable implementations and experiment on real software stacks and hardware platforms.

- **Talent development and teaching practice:** Jointly train developers through open-source internships, course labs, and technical exchange.

## 3. Join the Student Internship Program

RuyiAI offers students **on-site internship positions and online open-source internship programs**, helping students gain engineering experience on real projects, with compensation based on the role arrangement or program rules.

### On-site: AI Hardware-Software Co-Design Intern

RuyiAI offers on-site AI hardware-software co-design internships through a **hardware-software co-design collaboration between the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the Beijing Institute of Open Source Chip**.

This track is a good fit for students interested in AI system software, compiler optimization, computer architecture, and hardware-software co-design who want to participate deeply in R&D work.

You will have the opportunity to work on real project needs, including software development, hardware platform adaptation, performance analysis and optimization, and testing and validation—building a practical understanding of the relationships among AI models, system software, and hardware.

Specific internship directions and responsibilities will be determined jointly based on project needs and your interests and experience.

### Online: Open-Source Intern Growth Path

Through sustained open-source practice, we aim to help students progress from making a first contribution to participating in code review and project maintenance, growing into developers who can independently drive work and support community collaboration.

#### Getting Started: OSPP Pilot Program — Take the First Step as a Contributor

If you want a structured open-source experience, follow the **OSPP Pilot Program**.

You can start from community needs published by RuyiAI, learn about the project, identify problems, propose your own solution, and work with a community mentor to refine the proposal. Once selected, you will develop and deliver results around jointly agreed project goals, building complete open-source collaboration experience.

From understanding requirements and designing solutions to implementing code, writing tests, and producing documentation, you will grow into a project **Contributor** by solving real problems.

**Learn about projects and how to participate:** [RuyiAI OSPP page](https://summer.ospp.ac.cn/org/orgdetail/8d995d4c-b188-4690-9a53-c022dc7c19e3?lang=en)

Eligibility, application process, and project compensation follow the information published on the program page.

#### Next Level: Open-Source Maintenance Internship — Grow Toward Reviewer and Maintainer

After gaining contribution experience, you are welcome to join online open-source internships focused on **code review and project maintenance**.

At this stage, in addition to continued development, you can gradually take part in:

- **Code review:** Understand other contributors’ designs and implementations, and provide feedback on correctness, readability, test coverage, and maintenance cost.

- **Issue follow-up:** Help reproduce and analyze Issues, and drive localization, fixes, and verification.

- **Project maintenance:** Improve tests, documentation, and development workflows, and continually track the quality and evolution of related modules.

- **Community collaboration:** Join technical discussions and help new contributors understand the project and improve their submissions.

We encourage students to take on more responsibility through sustained contribution, growing toward **Reviewer** and **Maintainer** roles.

This growth comes from deep understanding of the project, steady contribution, and trust built through community collaboration. We look forward to working with you to pursue your areas of interest with depth and rigor.
